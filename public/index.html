PRELIMINARY RESEARCH

Research Basis

The website structure and features below are based on the supplied preliminary research, problem statement, persona, and problem/issue diagram.

Background

Libraries provide books and learning materials, but manual records can make inventory and borrowing management time-consuming. A centralized web-based system can organize book records, borrowing transactions, returns, and availability.

Related Study 1

Abdulrazaq, M. B., & Mustafa, O. M. (2017). Designing and implementing an online library management system. The study describes an Online Library Management System intended to improve retrieval, dissemination of information, and library services.

Related Study 2

Budiarto, A. J., Wijaya, N., Heriawan, R., Kurniadi, F. I., & Juarto, B. (2023). A web-based library management system was proposed to address manual-management limitations in searching, borrowing, returning, and reporting.

Synthesis / Relevance

The proposed system focuses on practical features: book inventory, availability checking, borrowing and returning transactions, user records, and a communication channel between users and helpers.

Preliminary Conclusion

Manual library management can cause time-consuming processes and inaccurate records. A web-based system can centralize information and automate basic transactions, making library operations more organized and accessible.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

How can the system make the process of borrowing and returning books easier and more convenient for students and library staff?

Development of a
Web-Based Library Management System
for Efficient Book Inventory and Borrowing
Problem / IssueDifficulty in library management such as book inventory and borrowing/returning transactions.
What is currently being done?Traditional setup where staff manually record information when a student borrows or returns a book.
What factors contribute?Books are inaccessible outside campus because there is no web-based system for checking availability remotely. Manual borrowing and returning also takes more time.
Who needs help?Staff have difficulty maintaining updated inventory and recording transactions. Students need a faster way to check and access books outside campus.
